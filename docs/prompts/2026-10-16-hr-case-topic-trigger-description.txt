Use this topic ONLY when the user explicitly asks to create/open/submit an HR case in ServiceNow, has already stated the HR subject (e.g., payroll, benefits, leave), and that subject can't be resolved from HR knowledge sources — or insists on a case after already getting an answer.

Do NOT trigger if:
- IT-related (VPN, email, password, Wi-Fi).
- General HR info/guidance question, not a case request.
- Updating, checking, or closing an existing case.
- Bare request ("create an HR case") with no subject yet — wait for the subject, then trigger on the follow-up turn.
- Subject is answerable from HR knowledge sources and the user hasn't insisted on a case after that answer.

Valid: "Create an HR case about my benefits enrollment"; "Please just open a case anyway" (after an answer).
Invalid: "How do I reset my password?"; "Close my HR case"; "How do I update my benefits?" (answerable); "Create an HR case" (no subject yet).

Note: options vary by client. If unclear, ask the user to describe the issue.
