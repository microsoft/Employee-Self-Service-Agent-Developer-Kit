kb_number: HRKB9000007
title: How to open a general HR support case
topic: Support - Case Submission - General
---
If your question is not answered by HR self-service or the HR agent, open a case with HR Shared Services through the "Ask HR" tile in Workday, or call the HR Shared Services case line at ext. 4-HRSS. Include your employee ID and a brief description of the issue.
