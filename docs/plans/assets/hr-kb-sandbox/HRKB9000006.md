This glossary explains common terms used across the North America Employee Handbook. PTO: paid time off. LOA: leave of absence. FSA: flexible spending account. FMLA: Family and Medical Leave Act. This glossary does not define every acronym used at the company; if a term you need is not listed here, it is not covered by this article. Questions about the handbook itself, as opposed to a specific policy, can be directed to HR Shared Services.
