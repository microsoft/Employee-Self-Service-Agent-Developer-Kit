title: Submitting a leave of absence (LOA) request
topic: Leave - LOA - Request Process
---
Submit a leave of absence request through the Time Off & Leave tile in Workday, selecting the applicable leave type and expected dates. Your manager and HR will be notified for review. If your LOA request has not appeared in Workday within 3 business days of submission, contact HR Shared Services at hr-shared-services@northerntrust.com or case line ext. 4-HRSS.
