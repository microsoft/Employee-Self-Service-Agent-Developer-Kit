---
kb_number: KB9000023
title: Clearing a paper jam on office printers (Part 2 - Resolution)
topic: Printer - Paper Jam
---
Open the indicated tray or rear panel gently and pull jammed paper in the direction of the paper path (never against it) to avoid tearing. Close all panels and press "Resume" on the display. If the jam error persists after two attempts, submit a ServiceNow hardware ticket.
