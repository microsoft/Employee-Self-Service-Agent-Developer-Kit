# Modify a topic

Already have a topic and want to change how it behaves? When you click **Modify a topic**, a chat opens where you describe the change in plain English. The kit will:

- Find the existing topic in your local working copy.
- Apply your requested change to the trigger phrases, conversation flow, or adaptive cards.
- Keep everything in sync with the integration (ServiceNow, Workday, or your custom system).
- Save the update locally — nothing is pushed to production until you run **Push**.

### Examples

> "In the PTO topic, also show the employee's accrued sick leave, not just vacation days."

> "Change the IT ticket topic so it asks for the building and floor before filing the ticket."
